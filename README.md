# Victus Journal

An intraday trading journal and workstation in a single file, `index.html`. No build step and no server: open the file in a browser (desktop or phone) and it runs.

## Layout

Every tab starts with its title and one line of filters, then one row of section tabs (a line under the open one); only the picked section shows.

**Look (v.132)**: one font (Inter) for text and numbers, cards with a hairline border and no shadows or gradients, sentence-case labels, and number rows drawn as one card split by thin lines instead of a box per number. Picked filter chips are a soft amber tint; solid amber is kept for the main buttons. Colours come from a few tokens (`--bg`, `--surface`, `--line`, `--txt`, `--mut`, `--dim`, `--acc` in `#clean-theme`), and the light theme only swaps them. On a phone the header is one line (logo, theme, balance) with the books under it.

- **Dashboard**: the **goal** (progress bar, its targets, to go today) and **Today** side by side on top. Today shows the day's P&L, a **Daily loss used** bar (today's loss against the daily loss limit: green, amber from 60%, red at 100%), risk used, trades and the max loss. Then the **month** (its picker and the 8 numbers), then the sections: **Equity & calendar** · **Day's trades** · **Week & grade** (week summary and process grade) · **Time & habits** (one short row per half-hour slot you traded, read down each column in time order, and the confidence levels, mistakes and what went right as rows: count, win rate, a bar for the size of the result and the money; clicking a row opens Day's trades with just those trades).
- **Signals**: **Load scanner alerts** on top (Paste signals and Delete all signals under **More**), then one filter line (Zone / Fib / Trendline switch, period, a **channel dropdown** that lists only channels with alerts, BTC day, **Exit at**, **Fees**) and the headline numbers (why some alerts have no result folds under one line), then **Alerts** · **Backtest report** · **Edge & breakdowns** · (**Fib levels**) · **Alert vs my trade** · **Market days**.
- **Analysis**: filters (book, period, BTC day), then **Overview** (Results by broker with a **Total** line, the size numbers, rule scorecard, size vs balance, mistakes) · **Review (N)** (trades from the last 30 days still to review, every book: ✓ Clean, pick a mistake, or Review) · **Position size** · **Trades** (trade by trade; the full list with prices and Edit folds under it) · **Market days** · **AI review**. A broker-report book (CoinDCX) adds **Report** first (its own numbers, equity, pairs and days) and its report trades on top of **Trades**: one row of sections, no tabs inside tabs. An empty book (an unused Dhan) is left out here as on the header bar. The "broke a rule" count in the numbers is the rule scorecard's, so the two always agree. Chart labels keep the year when the trades span more than one year.
- **Calculator**: account, then **Quick size** · **Full calculator** · **SL ladder** · **Goal planner**. Until you pick an account it sizes from a real book (the one picked on the header, else the one you traded last), never a made-up $5,000; an account you pick, Custom included, stays.
- **Settings** (was Sync & Settings): **Books & rules** (Trading Books, Setup Playbook, Trading Day) · **Connections** (GitHub sync, Delta, CoinDCX, AI Review key, Broker Report Files) · **Backup** (backup file, CSV, restore, purge, storage used).

## Tabs

- **Dashboard**: P&L, goals, time-of-day, confidence and mistake/positive tags. The **Review** section, now on Analysis (with the count waiting, e.g. "Review (5)"), lists every trade from the last 30 days not reviewed yet (no tag, rule, notes, confidence or Clean mark), journal and CoinDCX alike: tap **✓ Clean** if there's nothing to note, pick a mistake from the **Mistake…** dropdown (FOMO Entry, Early Exit, Moved Stop Loss, Revenge Trade) to tag it, or **Review** to open the inspector. Each tap takes the trade off the list. The week summary (P&L, win rate, avg R with the % move of those same trades, best setup, costliest mistake, P&L by day) is its own **Week & grade** section.
- **Process grade (weekly, no AI)**: under the week summary, an A–F grade for *how* you traded that week, not what you made, worked out from the trades with nothing to fill in. It lists stops used (yours or the alert's; trades with no stop known are counted, not hidden), size and loss inside your limits, reviewed trades with a mistake tagged, rules ticked, and revenge-sized trades with their cost. Three **behaviour scores** (0–100%) sit beside it, each with the last 8 weeks as small bars and the change from last week: **Discipline** (no revenge, size-up or trading past the daily limits; reviews without mistakes; rules ticked), **Risk management** (a stop known; size and loss inside your max risk) and **Consistency** (position size and risk % within half to 1.5× your usual for that book). A check with no data that week is left out instead of scoring 0. Grade: A 85%+, B 70%+, C 55%+, D 40%+, else F.
- **Revenge sizing**: a trade opened after 2 or more losses in a row that day (losses that had closed before it opened), with a position over 1.25× the average size of those losses. It is flagged in Analysis (Size & mistakes, rule scorecard's "calm" rule), breaks the "No revenge trades" goal streak, and is listed under the process grade with its P&L and the part of its loss that came from the extra size, plus a running 30-day total. Journal and CoinDCX trades alike.
- **Setup and level from the scanner's alerts**: every trade (journal and CoinDCX) is held against the scanner's fib and trendline alerts on its symbol, from when each was sent for a few candles of its timeframe (30m 1 day, 4h 4 days, 1D 10, 1W 30, 1M 60). It gets the setup (FIB or TRENDLINE, or the playbook setup of that name) and the level it was taken at: for a fib, the box edge its entry was nearest (L1-L4); for a trendline, SUPPORT (a long at the lower line), RESISTANCE (a short at the upper line), SP BROKEN (a short below the lower line, up to 3R under it) or RT BROKEN (a long above the upper line). The line's price at the entry minute comes from its two anchors, which the journal now keeps from the scanner's file. When nothing fits, the alert the trade was matched to (same symbol, side and day) still gives its setup, and a zone alert gives ZONE. A journal trade with no stop also gets its matched alert's stop (so its R and risk work). Only what was filled in automatically is changed later: a setup you picked, a level you ticked, or None stays. It runs when trades, alerts or the playbook change, and before a sync sends. The inspector shows "stop from alert" and "(from alert)". The scanner's fib and trendline alerts start on 29 Sep 2026, so older trades can't be placed.
- **Signals**: imported alerts by day, setup, score, outcome, symbol, channel, sector, timeframe and hour, with R and % move. Alerts stay on each device. The journal reads the scanner's files by itself when it opens, when Signals opens, when its tab comes back into view and every 10 minutes while it stays open (a failed read is tried again 2 minutes later, and the line under the title says so; new alerts wait to redraw while you're typing; from a week before the newest alert the device has, or further back to the oldest fib, trendline or 1D+ zone alert still open, up to 90 days, so a 1W or 1M result that closes weeks later still arrives) and adds new alerts and results, so recent trades always have alerts to match. Fib, trendline and 1D/1W/1M S/R zone results are priced like the scanner's report (out at +1R, or +2R when it hits 2R; Delta's 0.0826% fee for crypto, 0.1063% for NSE, the breakeven offset and the 0.05% stop slippage), and an alert already loaded is re-priced when that changes. 1D+ zones show under Zone alerts. **30m S/R zone alerts are not loaded or kept** (the scanner turned them off on 16 Oct 2026). Zone alerts from 1 Sep 2026 up to the rebuild's cutoff come from the scanner's **rebuilt zone backtest** (`zone_rebuild_*` files: every 4H / 1D / 1W / 1M zone alert today's rules would have sent, graded by today's scoring, fills within 15 min of US CPI / PPI / FOMC left out); zone alerts graded under older rules are dropped from the device when a rebuild arrives, and live zone alerts after the cutoff load as before. Fib and trendline alerts work the same way with the scanner's **rebuilt fib/trendline backtest** (`fibtl_rebuild_*`: every fib level and trendline touch today's rules would have sent since 1 Sep). **Load scanner alerts** is still there to load a longer range by hand. The line under the Signals title shows the newest alert and when it last checked. Under **Fib alerts**, a **Fib levels** section breaks the alerts down by level (L1-L4, worked out from each alert's fib): how many reached each level, alerts entering there, filled, hit +1R, win rate, total and average R, profit factor, best and worst; the same by how far price was from the entry when the alert fired; and your own trades on those alerts by the level your entry was nearest. The scanner enters fibs at L3 only, so L1, L2 and L4 show its daily what-if (`fib_level_results.json`): the same alerts as if entered at that level, with the same stop. It follows the period, channel and BTC-day filters. Your trades are held against the alerts: journal trades plus CoinDCX trades, whose side comes from the fills, or from the fees when the fills haven't come in yet (a trade from today). **My trades with no alert** lists every one in the period (paged only past 50). A **Backtest report** section plays every alert with a result in order, as if each was taken at 1R: win rate, expectancy, profit factor, average win and loss, payoff ratio, max drawdown and how long it lasted, recovery factor, win and loss streaks, spread (SD) and SQN, fill rate and alerts a day; an equity curve with its drawdown; the spread of results and how they ended (+2R, +1R, BE, SL); **What to cut** (groups of 5+ alerts that lost R, and what the rest made without them); **If you skip thin stops** (the same numbers keeping only stops at least 0.2% to 1% wide); and a breakdown by channel, symbol, side, stop width, score, hour, weekday, week, month or BTC day, sortable by any column. **Export CSV** saves the alerts in the view with their results. It follows the period, channel and BTC-day filters. **Exit at** (Scanner · 1R · 1.5R · 2R · 3R · 1% · 2% · 3%) re-scores every graded alert as if it closed at that one fixed target, with its own stop and no breakeven move: the headline numbers, calendar, Edge & breakdowns (By outcome then shows Target / SL / Time exit) and Alert vs my trade all follow it. Prices come from Delta's public candles (crypto only; NSE alerts have none there), from the alert until 20 candles of its own timeframe after the fill; a candle touching both the stop and the target counts as the stop, and a target not hit by then closes at that candle's close (Time exit). Costs as the scanner: 0.0826% round trip and 0.05% stop slippage. Finished alerts' candles are read once and kept on the device. **Fees** (With / Without) shows every result on the tab with or without the round-trip fee: the scanner's 0.0826% (Delta and CoinDCX's 0.02% maker in + 0.05% taker out + 18% GST; NSE 0.1063%), added back in R as fee % ÷ stop %. Headline numbers, calendar, Exit targets compared, every breakdown, the Backtest report and Fib levels follow it; stop slippage counts either way. Edge & breakdowns opens with **Exit targets compared**: the same alerts at every target side by side (win rate, total and average R, profit factor, total %), best marked; click a row to switch the tab to it. To keep the tab quick, the heavier sections (Backtest report, Edge & breakdowns, Fib levels, Market days) are drawn only when open, and each alert's result is worked out once per redraw.
- **Analysis**: deeper breakdowns, including Market days (BTC daily calendar, and hour by hour for a clicked day).
- **AI Trade Review** (Analysis tab): pick This month (default), Last month, 7 days or 30 days and press **Review my trades**. Every book's closed trades for the period (Delta, Dhan and CoinDCX report trades) go to Google Gemini with your book limits, playbook, totals by setup, level, side, symbol, hour and rule flag, and one line per trade (entry/exit, size × balance, P&L, R, stop, setup and fib level, tags, rules kept, flags, notes; the latest 400 at most). Before asking, the journal reads Delta's public candles (no key) around every crypto trade with prices (Delta trades, and CoinDCX trades whose fills give entry and exit; the symbol becomes Delta's, e.g. LINK/USDT → LINKUSD) and works out, per trade: the best and worst move while open (mfe / mae, in % and in R, or in 0.5% units with no stop), the share of the best move kept, minutes from the best price to the exit, and the best and worst move in the 1 h and 4 h after the exit. Each trade is labelled early_exit, cut_then_recovered, gave_back, held_loser or good_exit, with counts and the ₹ left by early exits, plus whether it came after a win or a loss that day. Finished trades' measures are kept on the device (`tapeAndTarget.aiPx`). It also adds a **Trailing SL** block (trades tagged trailed / moved SL / not tagged: wins, net, % of the best move kept, early exits, your R vs the VAS alert's own R on the same trades) and a **VAS alerts** block (fib, trendline and zone alerts sent in the period, taken vs skipped, by type, timeframe, side, score, scanner outcome and hour: alert R vs your R), so the AI can judge whether your TradingView check adds value and what to change in VAS. Each trade line names its alert, alert R, your R and stop handling. Gemini is told to go through every trade and alert and answers in short bullets (point ≤ 12 words, proof with numbers): **Verdict**, **What you do wrong**, **What works**, **Trailing SL**, **VAS alerts**, **Setup**, **Do this next**; the last review stays on screen on that device. Needs a free Gemini API key from aistudio.google.com, pasted in Settings → Connections → **AI Review (Gemini)** (model defaults to `gemini-2.5-flash`; when Gemini is busy (503) each model is retried after 3 s and 8 s, then `gemini-flash-latest`, `gemini-flash-lite-latest` and `gemini-2.5-flash-lite` take over; a missing model or the free limit moves straight to the next, and when any model was busy the message says Gemini is overloaded). **Copy for Gemini app** copies the same text to paste into the Gemini app with no key.
- **Broker reports**: upload an Excel (.xlsx) or CSV trade report, such as a CoinDCX futures report. The broker is read from the file (and can be changed before import) and gets a book of its own, next to Dhan and Delta. Its closed trades count everywhere a journal trade does: dashboard P&L, calendar, equity curve, goals and the book's balance, dated by the day they closed. Analysis shows them trade by trade (grouped from the report rows, with fees, funding, an equity curve and breakdowns by pair and day), with a "Results by broker" table to compare books. A broker book's **Size & mistakes** section runs the same size and mistake analysis as a journal book (rule scorecard, oversized trades, loss over limit, revenge and size-up after a loss, daily limits, fees that ate a win, position-size chart, trade by trade). A report has no prices, but CoinDCX's fee is a fixed share of each order's value (0.02% maker / 0.05% taker + 18% GST; 0.02% / 0.1% before Feb 2025; 0.01% + GST on gold and silver), so each trade's side and position value are worked out from its fees: the rates and side whose values add up to the report's gross P&L. Trades whose fees don't add up use the fills' prices, or show — for size. Balances before each trade are anchored to the CoinDCX wallet, as Delta's are to its live balance. The side also shows in the ledger and inspector. Its entry and exit (from the fills, once they come in) show in Day's trades and the All trades list too. Each report trade opens in the same Trade Inspector as a journal trade (from the Dashboard ledger, Signals, or the **Review** button in that table), with side and prices when the broker's fills give them; its review (rules kept, what went right, mistakes, notes, confidence) is kept with the journal (`repReviews`, synced), apart from the report's own numbers. Uploading the same report twice adds nothing twice. Uploaded files (and the CoinDCX API import) are listed, removed and uploaded in Settings → Connections → **Broker Report Files**, by broker.
- **Calculator**: position size and risk for NSE (INR) and crypto (USD) accounts. A linked book sizes from the same balance Trading Books shows (Delta live, CoinDCX wallet). **Quick size**: type a stop-loss and the risk to take; it shows the **capital used** (margin, your own money) and the **position size** at the leverage (₹1,000 at 5× = ₹5,000 position), plus the loss if the stop hits. The risk follows the header ₹ / $ switch: type ₹150, switch to $ and it becomes $150 ÷ the book's USD/INR rate, with every size shown in dollars.
- **Goal planner** (Calculator tab): type a target, the number of days and your trades per day; it shows what each day and each trade must make and the trades in all (₹600 in 2 days at 3 trades a day = ₹300 a day, ₹100 a trade, 6 trades). The target can be typed as an amount or as a **% of capital** (e.g. 10% of the balance); the box shows the other one under it, and switching converts what you typed. Pick a goal instead and it fills in what is still to earn and the trading days left. The win rate, reward : risk and position-size check sits folded under **Advanced**. The planner, like the whole calculator, follows the header's ₹ INR / $ USD switch; goals, balances and a linked book are converted to it.
- **Header**: stays on one line from tablet width up; on narrower screens the subtitle and "Balance:" word drop out and the book switcher scrolls sideways. The version pill stays on every width.
- **What's new**: tap the version pill next to the title to see every update, newest first: version, title, date, PR number and short bullets, with this build marked **Current**. Close, Escape or a tap outside shuts it. The list is `CHANGELOG` in `index.html`.
- **Goal profit as a % of capital**: in the goal editor (+ Add / edit on the goal card), the Profit target has a ₹ / **% of capital** picker. Pick %, type e.g. 10, and the goal aims for 10% of the books' balance when its window opened (a weekly goal: the balance on Monday), worked out again each new week or month as the capital grows or shrinks. The editor shows the amount under the box, and the card shows "· 10% of capital" next to the target. Switching back to ₹ turns the % into today's amount. Saved on the goal as `pnlPct` and synced.
- **Withdrawals**: the **Withdraw** button on the goal card logs money taken out to your bank (book, date, amount, note), and lists or removes past ones. A goal can have a **Money withdrawn (banked)** target, so it only counts as done once the profit is actually taken out. Withdrawals lower a book's worked-out balance (a live Delta or CoinDCX wallet already shows them) and sync like trades (`withdrawals` in `journal.json`).
- **Trade inspector**: click any trade, or its **Review** button in Analysis, to open it; tap a rule, setup-checklist item or tag to switch it on or off (saved at once). Pick the setup from the playbook's names (or None) in one tap; its checklist shows even before anything on it is ticked. For a levels setup (FIB, TRENDLINE) the list is its levels: ○ marks the ones it was not at, and tapping one makes it the only one.
- **Setup playbook** (Settings → Books & rules): each setup's rules are a checklist or, with **These are levels, not a checklist** ticked (FIB and TRENDLINE are by default), levels a trade is at one of. A checklist setup compares trades that kept every rule with ones that broke one; a levels setup shows each level's trades, win rate and P&L, and how many have no level. A line on top says how many trades have a setup, a level, and how many were filled in from alerts.
- **Settings**: books and rules, connections (Delta Exchange import, CoinDCX import, GitHub sync, Gemini key, report files), CSV export and backups. Trades come in from Delta and CoinDCX, so logging by hand is the small **+ Log trade by hand** link on the Trading Books card; the mobile bottom bar has no + button. A book with nothing in it (no trades, starting balance or withdrawals, like an unused Dhan book) is left off the book bar; Trading Books still lists it, marked "hidden from the bar (empty)".
- **CoinDCX API import**: CoinDCX doesn't answer web pages, so GitHub reads it for you. The sync repo runs `.github/workflows/coindcx.yml` every hour, which runs `tools/coindcx-import.mjs` from this repo with the key in the sync repo's secrets (`COINDCX_API_KEY`, `COINDCX_API_SECRET`). It adds new futures transactions (INR and USDT margined) to `journal.json`; each device gets them with its next sync, in the CoinDCX book an uploaded report fills. A transaction that is also in an uploaded report counts once (same pair and kind, within 3 seconds, same net). It also reads the fills (price, side and size) a week at a time, the first week ending tomorrow: CoinDCX stops at the start of the end day, so ending at today left today's fills out. GitHub skips many scheduled runs (3 to 6 hours apart on some days), so while the journal is open it starts the import itself once the last run is an hour old (checked through the sync token, which then needs **Actions: Read and write** as well as Contents), and syncs a minute later to pick up the new trades and wallet balance; without that permission the card says so and GitHub's own schedule (twice an hour) still runs it. A failed run says why on Settings → Connections → CoinDCX card. CoinDCX has no read-only keys, so use a separate key, not bound to an IP (GitHub's servers change address).
- **Trading day (05:30)**: the day rolls over at 05:30 IST (00:00 UTC, when exchanges reset their day), not midnight, so a trade at 01:30 counts for the day before on the dashboard, calendar, equity curve, daily loss and trade limits, goals, weekly review and Analysis (journal and broker-report trades alike). The trade keeps its real date and time; only the day it counts toward moves. Change it (midnight to 07:00) on the Settings → Books & rules → **Trading Day** card; it is saved on that device. An hour picked before (other than the old 2 AM default) is kept. The Signals tab and BTC market days stay on calendar days.
- **Rupees and dollars**: the ₹ INR / $ USD switch at the start of the accounts bar shows every book, tab and figure (P&L, fees, balances, limits, goals, broker reports) in one currency. Each book converts at its own rate: dollar books at Delta India's fixed ₹85, CoinDCX at its USDT price (₹99.30 by default, from its wallet ₹ ÷ $). Change a rate in Settings → Books & rules → Trading Books → Edit. Prices and sizes stay as traded; the trade inspector shows the book's own figure next to the converted one. Because the rates differ, an All accounts total in ₹ is not the $ total × one rate: a Delta loss and a CoinDCX win of the same dollars net to about zero in $ but to a profit in ₹ (the CoinDCX dollar is worth more rupees), so P&L, profit factor and return % can differ a little between the two. Set both rates the same to make them match. A goal's % of capital profit is taken from the capital in the currency shown, and the Today card's max risk on All accounts shows every book's (a range when they differ).

## Where data lives

- **This browser**: everything is saved in `localStorage` (`tapeAndTarget.v2` for the journal, `tapeAndTarget.signals` for alerts, `tapeAndTarget.mktHourly` for cached BTC candles). Clearing site data clears the journal.
- **Broker report uploads**: stored with the journal (`backtest` in `tapeAndTarget.v2` and in `journal.json` once something is uploaded): the report's rows only, never the cover sheet's name, email or PAN. Each uploaded file keeps the broker it belongs to; the broker's book (`rep-<broker>-<currency>`) is made from it on any device. The file is read in the browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Deleted records**: a deletion leaves a marker so sync doesn't bring the record back from another device. Markers are dropped after 30 days (183 for a deleted Delta trade's order tag, which stops the Delta import re-adding it), so `journal.json` stays small. A device not synced for over 30 days could bring back something deleted since.
- **GitHub sync (optional)**: in Settings → Connections, add a fine-grained token with *Contents: Read and write* on your sync repo (e.g. `victus2034/trading-journal`). The journal is saved there as `journal.json`, so other devices can pick it up. The token, Delta API key and webhook never leave the browser; the CoinDCX key lives only in the sync repo's Actions secrets.
- **Delta balance across devices**: the device holding the Delta key fetches Delta's wallet figure and syncs it as `deltaBalance` in `journal.json` (the figure only, never the key; sent when the journal opens, then when it moves, at most every 10 minutes, or hourly). A device without the key, or one Delta won't answer, shows that figure instead of its own trade sum; the header tag's tooltip says "fetched … on another device".
- **Gemini key**: kept only in this browser (`tapeAndTarget.gemini`), outside the journal, so it is never synced to `journal.json` or put in backups; the last AI review is kept in `tapeAndTarget.aiReview`. A review sends that period's trades (never any key or token) to Google.
- **Export**: Settings → Backup can download trades as CSV.

## Updating

Replace `index.html` and bump `<meta name="app-version">` near the top, so the version pill in the header shows which build each device is on. Add an entry for the new version at the top of `CHANGELOG` (same version, a short title, 1-3 bullets); the smoke test fails if the newest entry doesn't match the meta version.
Chart.js loads from jsDelivr; without it, charts are skipped and the rest still works.

## Checks

Every push runs `tests/smoke.mjs` on GitHub (the downloaded Chromium is cached between runs, and the job allows 25 min because GitHub's browser download is sometimes slow; the tests themselves stop at 10 min): it opens the page in a headless browser, with an empty journal and with made-up sample trades, on desktop and phone widths, opens every tab, and fails on any script error. It also uploads a made-up trade report (`tests/backtest-fixture.mjs`, as .xlsx and as .csv) and checks every trade and total against the report, in Analysis and on the Dashboard, plus the broker books and the per-broker results. To run it locally: `npm install`, `npx playwright install chromium`, then `npm test` (or `npm run test:twice`, which runs it twice; CI and the VS Code "Test twice" task use that). AI editors follow `AGENTS.md`: every change is tested twice before it is called done.

The clean-look check opens the sample journal and confirms the Daily loss used bar (a $1.00 loss on a $2.50 limit is 40% and green, $2.00 is 80% and amber), the dashboard tabs and their review count, the channel dropdown (only channels with alerts, and picking one filters), the Mistake… dropdown tagging a trade, and that no monospace font is left on the dashboard.

The what's-new check taps the version pill on desktop and phone and confirms the newest entry is this build and marked Current (once), versions only go down the list, every entry has bullets, the popup fits the screen, and Close, Escape and a tap outside shut it.
